Verdict
3.5 mm line-in on the master
Best. Tap the speaker / headphone jack / cheap splitter. Clean beat, no “whose mic.” Start here.
Mic on the master
Good backup when there is no jack. Put the box next to the speaker. Same as Govee’s sync brick. 

amazon.com

Bluetooth audio into the master
Tempting, painful. ESP32 as an A2DP sink is fiddly, latency and codec drama. Phone BT to a speaker, line-out or mic to the master, is simpler.
Phone mic in the app
Fine as a third mode. Phone has to stay in the room with the music.

Do not put a mic in every globe. They will disagree. Master hears once, broadcasts effect + beat clock over ESP-NOW.
