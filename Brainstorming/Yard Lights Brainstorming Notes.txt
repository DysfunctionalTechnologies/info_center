Short version on radio, then the numbers.ESP-NOW and sleepESP-NOW is just connectionless Wi‑Fi. Deep sleep turns the radio off. A packet from the house cannot wake a deep-sleeping stake. There is no LoRa-style “RX IRQ from master.”What you can do on ESP32-C3:Deep sleep (day / after midnight): ~5–20 µA. Wake on RTC timer and/or “solar panel voltage dropped” (dusk). Cannot hear the master.
Connectionless power-save (evening): esp_wifi_connectionless_module_set_wake_interval() + esp_now_set_wake_window(). Radio sleeps, then opens a short RX window on a schedule. Master must beacon in that window. Latency = interval (0.5–5 s is reasonable). Average radio current can be low if the window is short. 

documentation.espressif.com

Light sleep / auto light sleep: CPU paused, radio can still catch scheduled traffic. Wake in under ~1 ms. Higher current than deep sleep, fine for “glow hours.”

So: master cannot poke a dead-asleep light. It can talk to lights that have already decided it is evening and opened RX windows.That matches your plan:Panel voltage = day/dusk sensor. Optional NTP only on the indoor box.
Day: lights deep sleep, wake every 5–15 min, read panel + battery, go back down.
Dusk → midnight: MOSFET on, dim glow, radio in ESP-NOW power-save.
After midnight or low battery: MOSFET off, deep sleep until next dusk check.
Indoor box is always on. It is the Wi‑Fi  ESP‑NOW bridge and the time source. Lights never join home Wi‑Fi.

Can the slave units report back to the master?

Yes. ESP-NOW is two-way. Slaves can unicast a short packet to the master’s MAC whenever their radio is already up.They should not keep a session open. Report on the evening RX window, then sleep.What to sendKeep it under ~50 bytes:node id  
battery mV  
panel mV (or “day/dusk/night”)  
RSSI of last master beacon  
uptime / reboot reason  
current mode (glow / show / off)  
last error (boost fail, LED rail off, brownout)

That is enough for the phone UI: “light 3 is at 3.62 V, weak signal.”When they talkDo not stream. Piggyback on the schedule you already have:Master beacons every 1–2 s during 18:00–00:00.  
Slave wakes for its RX window, hears the beacon.  
Once per 5–15 minutes, slave replies with telemetry.  
After a show starts/stops, one extra ack is useful.  
Daytime: stay in deep sleep. No reports unless you wake for a panel check and the master happens to be beaconing (usually it shouldn’t by day).

If 8 lights each send 20 bytes every 10 minutes, the airtime is nothing.ReliabilityESP-NOW unicast can request an ack. Use that for telemetry. Broadcast is fine for “all lights, glow warm.”If a slave is at the far end of the yard, another slave can forward one hop. Don’t build a full mesh on v1. If light 7 can’t hear the house, put light 4 in between and allow “relay this 32-byte packet.” That is a later firmware flag, not a requirement for the first set.Power costA 20-byte TX is milliseconds at ~80–150 mA.
