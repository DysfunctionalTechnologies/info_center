ESP-NOW is actually a good fit here. A leak detector should not sit on Wi‑Fi. Deep sleep + GPIO wake + one short packet is how you get years on AAAs. People have already flashed Tuya leak pucks to ESP-NOW for that reason.

If it is just another AAA puck + cloud app, Govee already won on price and reviews.Hardware notesDetect: two stainless probes or a flex PCB on the belly. Optional 1 m extension for drip pans.
Latch the alarm until dry or muted from the phone. Old screamers that stop when you pick them up are annoying; ones that never mute are worse.
Debounce 1–2 s so a splash doesn’t page you.
Heartbeat every 12–24 h so “Unit C went silent” is a feature, same as the shade warning on the fairies.
Siren: a real piezo, 90–100 dB. The ESP32 DAC into a toy speaker will not cut it in a boiler room.
Power: 2×AAA or CR123, not a 18650. This device sits for years. ESP32-C3 deep sleep can do that if you are disciplined; a SuperMini with a power LED left on will not.

Honest takeBuild this if you already have the indoor master and want a second SKU that uses it. Do not build it as the first product. The fairy/globe thing is differentiated by look and behavior. This one is differentiated only if it is local, named, and lives in sheds and pump houses the consumer pucks don’t reach.Starter pack here is the same shape as the lights: master + 2 detectors, extra pucks sold alone.
